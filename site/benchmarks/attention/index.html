Benchmarks

Flash attention

Flash attention forward throughput in float16, causal and non-causal, against MLX's fused attention kernel.

What it measures

bench_attention runs the autotuned flash attention kernel from examples/08_flash_attention.py and compares it with mx.fast.scaled_dot_product_attention. The benchmark uses the following setup:

  • Shapes: batch 1 and 16 heads, with sequence lengths of 2,048 and 4,096 and head dimensions of 64 and 128. Each shape runs with and without a causal mask.
  • Data: float16 queries, keys, and values. MLX gets copies of the same data.
  • Configuration: the autotuner picks the block sizes and SIMD-group count on first use. The table lists the configuration as BLOCK_M×BLOCK_N and w SIMD groups.
  • Timing: Enceladus and MLX alternate in one process for 3 rounds of 10 runs. Each cell shows the best round's minimum and, in parentheses, the median of the round medians. MLX's wall-clock time adds about 0.1 ms, under 2% at these sizes.
  • FLOPs: the two matmuls, 4 × batch × heads × sequence length² × head dimension, halved for a causal mask.

Reading the results

  • Enceladus is within 5% of MLX everywhere. The eight shapes range from 4% faster to 5% slower, with a ratio from 0.96× to 1.05×.
  • Enceladus leads or ties at the smallest shapes. At sequence length 2,048 with head dimension 64, the two tie at 5.23 TFLOPS without a mask. With a causal mask, Enceladus is 4% faster by minimum time, and its median, 4.94, is 7% above MLX's 4.62.
  • MLX leads as the problem grows. At sequence length 4,096, MLX is 2-5% faster, with the largest gap at head dimension 128 without a mask: 5.04 against 5.29 TFLOPS. The wall-clock overhead is under 2% here, so this gap isn't a measurement effect.
  • Head dimension 128 runs slower than 64 for both implementations, for example 5.04 against 5.26 TFLOPS for Enceladus and 5.29 against 5.37 for MLX at sequence length 4,096 without a mask.
  • One configuration covers most shapes. The autotuner picked 64×32 with 8 SIMD groups for six of the eight shapes.
